About Us

VISION: To cultivate a thriving fellowship of pastors and ministers from the diaspora, committed to the Kingdom of God so as to be effective in the community. MISSION: To empower pastors and ministers from the diaspora through collaboration, support, and accountability. We are dedicated to fostering a spirit of unity among church leaders in the DFW area, encouraging personal and collective growth in faith. By creating opportunities for shared worship, prayer, and outreach, we strive to be a vibrant witness of Christ’s love and truth in our communities, working together to advance the Kingdom of God. CORE VALUES: 1. Spiritual Unity and Encouragement 2. Collaborative Fellowship 3. Mutual Accountability and Emotional Health 4. Unified Community Outreach
